"Simulated Gambling" are the two that matter.

That set lands at **12+**.

**Do not under-declare to chase a lower rating.** Misrepresenting here is its
own violation, and the User-Generated Content answer in particular is what makes
the report/block moderation coherent rather than decorative.

## The 16+ minimum and the 12+ rating disagree — on purpose

The questionnaire rates **content**; the terms set a **contractual minimum**.
Betta's content rates 12+; the terms require 16, and the database enforces it.

A reviewer may ask about this. The consistent answer: the 16 floor is a product
and data-protection choice (16 is the GDPR Article 8 ceiling for consent without
a parent), **not** a claim about content.

**A decision only you can make:** whether to raise the declared rating to 17+ to
match. Raising it is conservative and costs reach; leaving it at 12+ is
defensible and accurate about content. Either way nothing in the product
contradicts itself — the terms, the privacy policy and the sign-up screen all
say 16.
